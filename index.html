Happiness is a journey, not a destination

Anyone can benefit from therapy! Whether you are looking for help navigating your personal life stressors, looking to work with your partner to improve your relationship and deepen your connection, or looking for support for a child in your life, I am here to help.

My mission is to guide clients on a path of positive change and inner harmony. I am committed to restoring hope and confidence to those I serve through innovative therapy methods. I work with clients to help them gain insight into their actions and develop skills and techniques to create healthier outcomes. Each step of the journey is designed to inspire resilience, helping clients navigate life's challenges with confidence and balance.

I am here to meet you where you are and travel forward together! All care is trauma-informed, LGBTQIA+ affirming, and culturally sensitive. Meet me in Annapolis, Towson, online, or a hybrid of these!
